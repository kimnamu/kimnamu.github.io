About

I'm Jihoon Kim, an AI/ML Delivery Consultant. I help enterprises take generative AI from promising demos to systems that actually hold up in production — the part where data quality, permissions, cost, evaluation, and governance decide whether a project ships or stalls.

This site is where I write the field notes I wish existed: what breaks between proof-of-concept and production, what the numbers actually say when you benchmark instead of guess, and how to put autonomous agents to work without losing control of them.

What I write about

  • Production agents on AWS — Bedrock, AgentCore, and the operational reality behind the demos
  • Evaluation & observability — how to trust systems that are non-deterministic by design
  • Cost, latency, and model-selection trade-offs, backed by real measurements
  • Governance for AI that takes action — and now, AI that spends money
